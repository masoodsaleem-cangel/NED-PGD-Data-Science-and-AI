## Author
Masood Saleem

## Features
- ✅ Auto-generate Patient ID (P00001-2026 format)
- ✅ Add/Modify/Delete/Search Patients
- ✅ Record Vitals (BP, Sugar, Temp, Weight, Height, BMI)
- ✅ Blank entries allowed for optional vitals
- ✅ Historical visit tracking with timestamps
- ✅ Individual patient charts
- ✅ Export to Excel (multi-sheet)
- ✅ Data persistence with CSV files
